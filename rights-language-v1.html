What brands are getting, exactly

The core definition. This block is reused everywhere — emails, the license recap, and eventually the portal.

Already included · free
Every campaign
no extra cost, already theirs
  • Reshare any campaign post on the brand's own social channels
  • Use it organically — website, emails, blog — for 30 days after it goes live
Buying the rights
Per post, one-time
$[75] — paid to the creator, nothing added on top
  • The raw, unwatermarked video file (plus the posted version)
  • Perpetual, worldwide license for the brand's owned channels: website & product pages, email, organic social — no expiration
  • Paid ads for 12 months from purchase — renewable after that (we re-up with the creator)
  • Light edits allowed — trim, crop, captions, logo overlay, re-cuts for ad formats
  • The creator's original post stays live — the organic social proof keeps working
  • Benable handles everything — we take each post to its creator for sign-off, then handle the license, payment, and file delivery. Only creator-confirmed posts are billed
Not included (say only if asked): exclusivity — the creator can keep working with other brands — resale or transfer of the content to third parties, and edits that change how the creator looks or what they say. The license is for the brand's own channels.
If they ask about boosting from the creator's handle — TikTok Spark Ads / IG partnership ads need a creator authorization code. Answer: "we can arrange that too."
Why ads cap at 12 months — note for Katie & Tony. Still one price, one yes: the ads term is the only clock, and raw files + owned channels stay perpetual (nobody renegotiates a product page). Twelve months clears every known benchmark — Chiara's ask was "at least 90 days" (this is 4×), Statusphere's published ads license is 1 year, and it's the industry's default clock (IZEA's offer builder defaults to 12 months; Aspire's picker is 3/6/12; TRIBE's is 12-or-perpetual). It kills the creator-risk case ("my face in ads forever for $75") exactly where market pricing runs per-30-days. And when productized, the 12-month renewal becomes the paid-extension moment (Amazon's model: capped license + preset extension rate) — while in the manual phase a 1-year clock means zero renewal admin and no expiry landing right before BFCM.

06 —

Decisions before it goes out

Five calls to make — recommendations marked. Once these are set, every orange chip above gets its real value.

1

The rate

Band agreed with Katie: $50–100. Tony's anchors: he's told brands "generally around $50," and the PCA note expected ~$40 average.

recommend

$75 flat per video ($50 photos). Creator-generous is right when yes-rate matters more than margin. Use $50 to match Tony's prior quote exactly.

2

Pass-through vs margin

Tony's contract language already promises "no extra charges on our end."

recommend

100% pass-through for the manual phase — the learning is the product. Add margin when productized.

3

Perpetual vs time-boxed

Aug 3 framing was perpetuity; market norms price ads per-30-days and 12 months is the industry default clock (IZEA, Aspire, TRIBE).

decided aug 6

One bundled price. Owned channels + raw files perpetual; paid ads capped at 12 months, renewable. See the note for Katie & Tony above.

4

Creator payment rail

Needed before Text 3 goes out. At $50–100/creator this sits well below tax-reporting thresholds — flag to accounting only once it scales.

decide

Venmo or PayPal — pick one (or offer both, as drafted).

5

Brand billing

How the $[75] × n reaches the invoice.

recommend

Add to the next subscription invoice (as drafted). Alternative: one-off Stripe invoice per purchase.
