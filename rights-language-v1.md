## 1 · What the brand is getting (the definition — reuse everywhere)

**Included in every campaign already (free):**
- Reshare any campaign post on the brand's own social channels, and use it organically (website, emails, blog) for 30 days after it goes live.

**When they buy the rights to a post — $[75] one-time, paid to the creator:**
- The **raw, unwatermarked video file** (plus the posted version).
- A **perpetual, worldwide license** for the brand's owned channels: website and product pages, email, and organic social. No expiration.
- **Run it as paid ads for 12 months** from purchase — renewable after that (we re-up with the creator).
- **Light edits allowed** — trim, crop, captions, logo overlay, re-cuts for ad formats.
- The **creator's original post stays live** on their page, so the organic social proof keeps working.
- **Benable handles everything**: we take each post to its creator for sign-off, then handle the license, payment, and file delivery. No fees added on top, and only creator-confirmed posts are billed.

**Not included** (say only if asked): exclusivity (the creator can keep working with other brands), resale/transfer of the content to third parties, and edits that change how the creator looks or what they say — the license is for the brand's own channels.

**Optional add-on to mention if asked:** boosting from the creator's own handle (TikTok Spark Ads / IG partnership ads) needs a creator authorization code — we can arrange that too.

### Why ads cap at 12 months (note for Katie & Tony)

- **Still one price, one yes.** The bundle doesn't change — the ads term is the only clock in the deal. Raw files and owned channels (site, email, organic social) stay perpetual, because nobody renegotiates a product page.
- **It clears every known benchmark.** Chiara asked for ad rights "for at least 90 days" — that's her floor, and 12 months is 4× it. Statusphere's published paid-ads license is 1 year. And 12 months is the industry's default clock: IZEA's offer builder literally defaults to 12 months, Aspire's brief picker offers 3/6/12, TRIBE's license picker is 12-months-or-perpetual.
- **It removes the creator-risk case.** "My face ran in ads forever for $75" is the complaint that could poison the creator side; ads is exactly where market pricing runs per-30-days. Bounded-at-a-year kills it while costing brands nothing they've asked for.
- **It sets up the future revenue line.** When this is productized, the 12-month renewal becomes the paid-extension moment (Amazon runs 90-day licenses with a preset extension rate). In the manual phase, a 1-year clock also means zero renewal admin for us — and no brand hitting an ads expiry right before BFCM.

## 5 · Decisions to make before sending

1. **The rate.** Band agreed with Katie: $50–100. Suggest **$75 flat per video** (simple, mid-band; could do $50 for photos). Two anchors from Tony: he's told brands rights run "generally around $50," and the PCA note expected an average of ~$40/creator — so $75 is generous to creators, which is right for the first manual deals where yes-rate matters more than margin. If you'd rather match Tony's prior quote exactly, use $50.
2. **Pass-through vs margin.** Recommend **100% pass-through** for the manual phase — it matches the language Tony already put in the PCA/Just Juice contracts ("no extra charges on our end"), it's a great sales line, and the learning is the product. Add margin when it's productized.
3. **Perpetual vs time-boxed — DECIDED (Aug 6).** One bundled price; owned channels + raw files perpetual; **paid ads capped at 12 months, renewable**. See the "why 12 months" note above.
4. **Creator payment rail.** Venmo or PayPal — decide before Text 3 goes out. (At $50–100/creator this is well below tax-reporting thresholds; worth a flag to accounting only once it scales.)
5. **Brand billing.** Easiest: add to next subscription invoice (as drafted above). Alternative: one-off Stripe invoice per rights purchase.
